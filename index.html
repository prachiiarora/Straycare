About StrayCare 💚

Every animal deserves a chance.

StrayCare is a community-focused animal rescue prototype designed to connect people who spot animals in need with volunteers and rescue organisations.

Our mission is to make reporting, rescue coordination and recovery tracking easier.

Our Mission

🐾 Faster reporting

❤️ Compassionate care

🤝 Community participation

🏥 Better recovery tracking

Important Notice

This is currently a demonstration application. Reports are saved on this device only. No real rescue team or emergency service is contacted.
